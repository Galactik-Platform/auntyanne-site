Waitlist privacy notice

This covers only the founding families waitlist. Using the service itself has its own, fuller privacy notice. Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Who we are

Aunty Anne is run by Galactik Ltd (company 17104671), 133 Chase Side, London N14 5HD. We have applied to register with the Information Commissioner’s Office (application reference C2089512). Contact: AuntyAnne@galactik.tech.

What we collect

AlwaysYour first name and email address, and that you agreed to hear from us (with the date and time).
If you chooseWho Cara would ring (for example “my mum or dad”) and your postcode area (the first part only, like N14).
NeverAnything about anyone’s health, or the name or number of the person Cara would call.

Why, and on what basis

To email you when your founding-family place is ready, and with a few updates before launch. Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box. We also use the optional answers, counted up and anonymised, to plan where and how many people to help set up.

Who sees it

Only us, and two services that work for us: Supabase, which stores the list on servers in London, and Resend, which sends our emails from Ireland. Both are bound by data processing agreements. We never sell or share the list, and we don’t use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe, or 12 months after Aunty Anne launches, whichever comes first. Then we delete it. The unsubscribe link in our emails deletes your details straight away. If you become a customer, the main privacy notice applies from then on.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe with the link in any email (including the confirmation we send when you join), or ask us to see, correct or delete your details, by emailing AuntyAnne@galactik.tech. We’ll reply within one month. Withdrawing consent doesn’t affect anything we did before.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your details, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.
