How it works

Here’s how Aunty Anne will work when your place is ready.

  1. You set it up

    Tell us who Cara should ring and when.

  2. They decide

    On the first call Cara explains who she is and asks if they’d like her to ring again. No means no.

  3. You stay in touch

    After each call you get a short note, like “a lovely chat about the garden”. Not the recording.

Play with Cara: family game night, hosted by Cara

Put one phone, tablet or laptop in the middle of the table and Cara hosts a quick word game for everyone, grown-ups and grandparents included. She asks everyone’s name first. No scores, no wrong answers.

  • Name Three Things
  • This or That
  • Finish the Saying
  • What Rhymes With

One microphone hears the whole room, and Cara can’t tell voices apart, so she’ll sometimes ask who said what.

The honest bits

Cara is a computer
She’s warm and patient, but she isn’t a person, and she never pretends to be.
“Stop” always works
If the person Cara calls says stop, the calls stop. Only they, or someone with legal authority for them, can start them again.
Not an emergency service
If something sounds wrong, Cara tells them to call 999 and lets the family contacts you choose know.
Never asks for money
Cara never asks for money, bank details or passwords. Anyone who does isn’t Cara.
